    Your cards

    The API sends a tag number masked to its last four characters, and this page shows no more than that. A slot is the card's position in the door controller's own table, and it is never reassigned, because a slot is a physical address.

    No card is recorded for you. Card access is decided by a vote of card members, and the card access view says what that involves.

      Certifications

      A certification is per tool. Certification is required before you use the tool it covers. A revoked one stays on this list with its date. A certification can be revoked and granted again later, and that history is what an instructor needs to see.

      Nothing is recorded for you yet. An instructor for a tool records a certification after they have run you through it.

        Waiver

        This system records that you signed a waiver, when, and where the document is kept. It holds nothing that is on the document. The lab already keeps waivers somewhere, and a second copy would be a second thing to protect.

        No waiver is recorded for you. That is an answer rather than a fault: it means nobody has written one down. An admin records it once you have signed.

          Directory

          Members who chose to be listed, showing only what each of them chose to show. Reading it needs nothing more than being a member. It is not gated on orientation: the legacy application gated it on an admin having typed a date into a field, which locked paying members out for a volunteer's inaction.

          Nobody is listed. Every member has chosen not to appear, which is theirs to choose.
