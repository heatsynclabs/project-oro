    Your cards

    The API sends a tag number masked to its last four characters, and this page shows no more than that. Where a card sits inside the door controller is the door service's business, so it is not on this page and never reaches a browser.

    No card is recorded for you. The card access view says what being nominated involves.

    CardTagStateIssuedRevoked

    If you lose a card, tell an admin and they revoke it. Issuing one is an ordinary admin action, and what authorises it is a vote of card members at Hack Your Hackerspace.

    Certifications

    A certification is per tool. Certification is required before you use the tool it covers. A revoked one stays on this list with its date, because a certification can be revoked and granted again later, and that history is what an instructor needs to see.

    Nothing is recorded for you yet. An instructor for a tool records a certification after they have run you through it.

    Waiver

    This system records that you signed a waiver, when, and where the document is kept. It holds nothing that is on the document. The lab already keeps waivers somewhere, and a second copy would be a second thing to protect.

    No waiver is recorded for you. That is an answer rather than a fault: it means nobody has written one down. An admin records it once you have signed.
